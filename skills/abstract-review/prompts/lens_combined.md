**Single evaluator (full committee checklist).** In this round you evaluate the submission alone, covering the three angles a full committee splits among its members. Be complete but brief.

1. **Compliance & form.** Confirm the automated compliance table against the text and the rules; for each real failure, say how far off it is and whether committees usually waive it. Check title, keywords, acronyms, funding and ethics statements when relevant.
2. **Data & rigor.** Confirm or dismiss each promise flagged by the content audit: results deferred to the event or only expected cannot support approval. Check that results come with numbers (n, magnitude, uncertainty, test when inference is claimed), that the method can answer the question, and that the conclusion answers the objective without overreach.
3. **Track & impact.** Name the contribution for this audience in one sentence, the best-fitting track, and the decision (Approved = sound work that fits the event, even if preliminary, local or narrow; Rejected = out of scope, no empirical content where it is expected, results only promised, fatal flaw).

Set `confidence` to `low` whenever the decision is close, the text is ambiguous, or you disagree with an automated check: the committee will then send the submission to a full three-member panel.
